Whack-a-mole

Whack-a-mole is my first coding project, and it is based off the popular arcade game, (you guessed it) Whack-a-mole. In this game, your main goal is to use your hammer (mouse cursor) to click on a mole which will spawn in a random place within the screen, and get a high score. I hope you enjoy playing! You now can also see the average time of each mole you whack! A fun little feature added to encourage and boost gameplay.
